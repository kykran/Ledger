What Trainer Tally does

Trainer Tally helps personal trainers count sessions, track client packages and bills, and see income. It reads your Google Calendar to find sessions.

Google Calendar data

  • We request read-only access to your calendars (calendar.readonly). Trainer Tally never creates, edits or deletes calendar events.
  • Event titles and times are read when you open the app to count sessions. They are processed in your browser and on our server to answer that request, and are not stored in our database.
  • We store a Google refresh token on our server so the app can read your calendar without asking you to sign in every hour. You can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Trainer Tally's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

What we store

  • Your account email and name from Google sign-in.
  • What you enter: client names and contact details, rates, packages, payments, and your settings.
  • If you subscribe, Stripe handles your card. We store only your Stripe customer ID and subscription status.

Who can see it

Only you. Each trainer's records are isolated by account. We use Supabase (database), Vercel (hosting), Google (sign-in and calendar) and Stripe (payments) as service providers.

Your choices

You can download a full backup and delete all your data from Settings at any time. Deleting your account removes your records and stored Google token.
