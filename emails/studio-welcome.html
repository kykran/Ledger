The short version: your trainers keep booking on your shared Google Calendar like they do now. Trainer Tally reads that calendar, figures out who trained when, and works out what each trainer owes in rent each month. No spreadsheets, and no chasing people for their session counts.

Set up your studio → Or try the demo first

Getting set up

1
 Sign in with Google. Use the account that owns or can see your studio calendar. Trainer Tally only ever reads it. Nothing on your calendar is changed.
2
 Pick your studio calendar and tell it about your space: how many trainers can work at once, and your open hours.
3
 Set your default rent. Per session (e.g. $20), a flat monthly rate (e.g. $660), or none. You can give any trainer their own rate later.
4
 Add your trainers. Everyone who books on the calendar shows up under Trainers → Found on the calendar, matched by the Google account they book with. Tap Add as trainer, then set their rent.
5
 Invite them to link up (optional, but worth it). Tap Get invite code next to each trainer and send them the link. Once they join, they see their rent statement in their own Trainer Tally app.
 

Each month

Rent adds up as sessions happen. The Overview shows rent billed so far, a projection for month end, and who still owes what.

Send statements and log payments. Each trainer has a ready-to-send statement. When they pay (Venmo, Zelle, cash, whatever you use), log it in one tap and their balance updates.

Trainers sign off. Two days before the month ends, linked trainers see your numbers next to their own calendar count and tap Confirm or Dispute with a note. Once they confirm, the month is locked, so there's nothing to argue about later.

What's inside

Overview: rent this month, outstanding balances and sign-offs

Trainers: rent rules, sessions, balances and invites

Room usage: how full you are by day and hour

Revenue: billed vs. collected, plus a spreadsheet download

Trends: which trainers are growing and which are slowing down

A few tips

•  Each trainer books with their own Google account. That's how sessions are matched to the right person. If someone books for a trainer, use Same as… to link that account to the trainer.

•  Cancellations don't count. Events with words like “cancel” or “blocked” in the title are skipped. You can edit the list in Settings.

•  Off-site sessions are free. A session titled like “Sarah home” or “Mike online” is still counted but owes no rent and doesn't take up a room.

Private by design

Calendar access is read-only. Trainers who link up keep their rates, clients and income private. The studio only sees its own calendar, the rent it works out, and each trainer's monthly confirm or dispute.
